Privacy Policy

Sai Jyothsna Caterers & Suppliers · Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address when you create an account, and your password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Google sign-in — if you choose "Login with Google", Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with us so we can create and sign you in to your account. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access any other Google data.
  • Booking details — the event information you enter when booking: event type and date, venue address, number of guests, food and service selections, and any notes you add.
  • Contact details — your phone number when you provide it for a booking or an inquiry, so we can reach you about your event.
  • Reviews — the rating and comment you submit, shown under your name.
  • Technical details — a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a security cookie that protects our forms. We do not use advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To take, confirm, and manage your catering bookings and quotations.
  • To contact you about your booking or your inquiry.
  • To keep the website secure and working correctly.

How your data is stored and shared

Your information is stored in a secure database hosted by our service providers (website hosting and database hosting). We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We share it only when needed to run this website — for example with our hosting providers — or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and booking records for as long as your account exists or as needed for our business records. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time (see below).

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data. To do any of these, contact us at pottahindu@gmail.com or call +91 97008 73454. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is intended for adults booking catering services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
